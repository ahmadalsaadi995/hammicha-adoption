About Hammicha

Hammicha’s story is one of resilience and transformation. She was adopted as a tiny kitten from the streets of Amman in October 2024. When she was first found, she was frightened and bullied by the local street cats. Since then, she has blossomed in a loving home, learning what it means to be safe, warm, and cherished.


Today, she is a gorgeous orange and white tabby with classic "orange cat" energy—she is incredibly playful, curious, and endlessly entertaining. She is a professional sunbather, a dedicated toy hoarder (she especially loves her little blue plushie and her toy mouse), and an expert at finding the highest cabinet in the room to survey her kingdom from.


She loves interactive play, being carried around (showing off her adorable pink toe beans), and will reward you with endless entertainment. She is fully litter trained, vaccinated, and spayed.


Why she needs a new home: Because of her high energy and playful nature, Hammicha would thrive best in an adult-only home or with older children. She doesn't quite understand that human babies are fragile, and she deserves a calm, patient environment where she can be the center of attention.
